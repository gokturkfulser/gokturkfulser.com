Kelime Avı is a small puzzle game where you try to guess a new word every day. It plays in Turkish, and the Turkish-specific letters (ç, ğ, ı, i, ö, ş, ü) are at its centre: <strong>İ</strong> and <strong>I</strong> are different letters, and upper-casing follows Turkish rules.

## How to play

1. Guess a five-letter word.
2. After each guess, look at the colour of the letters: teal means the right spot, orange means the letter is in the word but elsewhere, dark grey means it is not in the word.
3. Find the word within six tries.

A new word arrives every day; once you finish, you can keep playing with random words as long as you like.

## Notes

It works with a keyboard or a touch screen: type the letters or tap the on-screen keyboard, and Enter submits the guess. The game runs full screen on its own page and can also be played right here; if your reduced-motion preference (`prefers-reduced-motion`) is on, the letter-flip animations are turned off. Today's guesses are stored in your browser, and no other data is collected.

Guesses are checked against an openly licensed Turkish dictionary; a word that is not in it is rejected and does not cost you a try. The word of the day comes from a curated list of common, uninflected words.
