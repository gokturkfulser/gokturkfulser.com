Box scores tell you who scored and who rebounded. They don't tell you whose games are worth putting on. The best measure I could find for that is the NBA's own pick: after every game night, the league ranks the night's ten best plays in a single video.

Scoring works like this: the night's No. 1 play earns 10 points and No. 10 earns 1. On nights when only five plays make the cut, No. 1 still earns 10. When a play is a two-man job, like an alley-oop or a block that turns into a fast-break dunk, the points are split, with the bigger share going to whoever made the play special.

The table updates every morning with the previous night's video.
